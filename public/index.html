The architect's voice, 2 to 30 seconds: upload an MP4, record it here, or type the words. The video follows it word for word.

  • Record in a quiet room with the phone close to the mouth.

  • Clip length and camera cuts follow the recording automatically.
  • Use a quiet room and keep the phone or microphone close to the mouth.
